🗓️Booking Help

How far in advance should I book?

Advance vs Same-Day Bookings

For outstations like Goa or Gokarna, we recommend booking 12–24 hours ahead to secure your vehicle choice. Same-day emergency dispatches are supported subject to availability.

🧾Pricing Policy

Are tolls and driver allowances included?

No Hidden Costs

All state permits, tolls, and driver bata can either be packaged as an all-inclusive fixed price or clearly itemized upfront. There will never be surprise demands at the end.

🚗One-Way Available

Can I book a one-way cab without return charges?

One-Way Drop Service

Yes! We offer dedicated one-way drop fares to Goa, Belagavi, Gokarna, and Bengaluru without forcing you to pay for the vehicle's return journey.

❄️AC Guaranteed

Will the AC work properly on Ghat roads?

Vehicle Comfort

Yes. All our sedans and SUVs feature fully operational air conditioning across hill routes like Dandeli, Chorla Ghat, and Anmod Ghat.
